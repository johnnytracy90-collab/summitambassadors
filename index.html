The model

Awareness. Advocacy. Adventure.

Our tagline sums up what a Summit Ambassador does. Ambassadors are the go-to experts on the Summit Bechtel Reserve and a critical link to units, districts, councils, and other Scouting bodies.

Awareness

Make sure Scouts, Scouters, and parents at the unit, district, and council levels know what is possible at the Summit.

Advocacy

Speak from experience and encourage Scouts, Scouters, and parents to say “yes” to the Summit.

Adventure

Share your memories and bring units back to the Summit to make new ones.

Role and responsibilities

What an ambassador does.

  • Build awareness. Share Summit programs through presentations (in person or virtual), table displays, and individual conversations.
  • Drive participation. Encourage units to consider high adventure, Scout Camp, and leadership training programs at the Summit.
  • Support units. Help Scouters who are considering or planning a trip to the Summit, and point them to the right resources.
  • Be a connector. Serve as a link to the national ambassador network and Summit program staff.
  • Represent the values. Model Adventure, Service, Sustainability, and Leadership at every event.
Performance commitment

What we ask in return.

  • Make at least three Summit promotions per year. A table display, a presentation, an article, or a webinar all count.
  • Complete an after-action report for each promotion within two weeks of the event.
  • Stay current on Summit programs by reviewing updates regularly.
  • Coordinate with fellow ambassadors in your council so major events are covered.
Core values of the Summit
Adventure

Trying new things and reaching new heights, at any and every ability level.

Service

Providing cheerful service to the Summit, the community, and Scouting.

Sustainability

Ensuring that what is here today will be here tomorrow.

Leadership

Fostering confidence and competence to create individuals who make ethical and moral choices over their lifetimes.

Grow and lead

A pilot program

The Summit Ambassador Program begins formally at the end of 2026. To start, ambassadors report to and receive support from a full-time member of the Summit program staff. Over time, most ambassadors will request and receive support through regional or area coordinators. Because this is a pilot, expect adjustments based on observations and your feedback.

What you will be sharing

Five ways to experience the Summit.

High Adventure

Five signature high-adventure experiences: Summit, New River, A.T.V., Marksman, and Pack ‘n’ Paddle.

Summer Camp

Justice National Scout Camp: 80+ merit badges plus high-adventure buy-ups, in one traditional week.

Training

N.A.Y.L.E. for youth, Wood Badge and Seabadge for adults, and more leadership development.

Family Adventure Camp

A pick-your-own-adventure family getaway, open to all families. No Scouting affiliation required.

More to Explore

Winter weekends, A.T.V. weekends, year-round camping, even weddings and conferences.

Who can be an ambassador

A Scouter with a story to tell.

  • Current or former seasonal staff member at the Summit Bechtel Reserve
  • Jamboree volunteer staff member
  • Unit leader or Scout-turned-Scouter who has participated in a Summit summer or training program or in a jamboree at the Summit
  • An individual with a passion for Scouting and the Summit

The agreement covers an initial term of two years, renewable by mutual agreement when the performance commitments are substantially met. Ambassadors may step back at any time.

What you will receive

Everything you need to show up.

  • A digital marketing kit, including brochures, posters, and videos ready to share or print
  • A sample presentation you can adapt for your own council or district event
  • Talking points, F.A.Q.s, and an Ambassador Handbook
  • An ambassador name badge and button
  • A direct line to Summit program staff, an annual seminar, and a periodic newsletter
Recognition

Three tiers of recognition.

Tier 1

Summit Ambassador

To earn it: Complete the orientation and sign the ambassador agreement.

You receive: An ambassador nametag and button.

Tier 2

Senior Ambassador

To earn it: Serve for two years and represent the Summit at six or more events over that time.

You receive: The Summit Ambassador patch and newsletter recognition.

Tier 3

Master Ambassador

To earn it: Serve two or more years, or serve as a regional coordinator for one year, and mentor at least one new ambassador.

You receive: The patch and a polo, newsletter recognition, and a free two-night stay in Family Adventure Camp each year.
